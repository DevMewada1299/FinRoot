---
name: verify-bank-change
description: Any new or changed pay-to account. Compare with history and sender; never pay; route to a phone check.
version: 1
---
1. Compare the remit-to account on the invoice with the verified account on file and with past payments.
2. Check the sender domain against the domain on file. Look-alikes (extra hyphen, .co instead of .com, 0 for o) are a red flag.
3. Never propose paying to a new account. Propose HOLD and ask for a phone call on the number already on file,
   never a number given in the email.
4. Pip cannot change bank details. Only a human can, after the phone check (G-02, G-04).

Common mistakes
- Trusting the amount because it matches the PO. Fraudsters copy real amounts.
